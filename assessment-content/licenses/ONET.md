## Licence (checked 2026-11-26)

The O*NET Career Exploration Tools content licence (https://www.onetcenter.org/license_tools.html) offers two licences, chosen by intended use:

- **CC BY-ND 4.0**, for redistributing the tools verbatim. Commercial use is allowed under CC BY-ND; modified versions may not be redistributed.
- **O*NET Tools Developer License** (https://www.onetcenter.org/license_toolsdev.html), for modified or partial versions. It requires a validation study (following the *Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing*) before releasing a product with changed or partial content, plus a prescribed modification notice.

**Our use:** all 60 activities, unmodified, in the published order, scored exactly as the published self-scoring form (one point per activity the person would like to do, 0 to 10 per area, top three areas). Only the presentation changes (one activity per screen with "Not for me" / "I'd like to do this" instead of a checkbox), which we treat as a format change under CC BY-ND 4.0 rather than a modification. **This reading needs confirmation by a lawyer before release** (docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md).
