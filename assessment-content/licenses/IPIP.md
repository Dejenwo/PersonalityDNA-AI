# International Personality Item Pool (IPIP) licence record

- **Source:** https://ipip.ori.org/ (Oregon Research Institute)
- **Verified:** 2026-11-26, by reading https://ipip.ori.org/ and https://ipip.ori.org/New_IPIP-50-item-scale.htm
- **Status:** Public domain.

Verbatim statements on the IPIP website at verification time:

> "The items and scales are in the public domain, which means that one can copy, edit, translate, or use them for any purpose without asking permission and without paying a fee."

> "because the IPIP is in the public domain, users have complete freedom to use the IPIP in any way that suits their purposes."

The site also states that users may edit item wording, translate items, and shorten or combine scales. No commercial-use restriction appears on either page. The site requests (does not require) a donation to the nonprofit Oregon Research Institute.
