## Hypotheses (to test, not facts)

- H1: People who finish a free Big Five test want "what do I do with this", which today's free tests rarely answer.
- H2: Transparent methodology and no-diagnosis language increase trust compared with type-based tests.
- H3: Grounded AI coaching tied to one's own results is a stronger reason to subscribe than one-off reports.
- H4: Pricing in the master prompt ($9.99/month, $69.99/year) is plausible for the category; to be tested.

## Rules for adding findings

Each finding records: claim, source URL or interview ID, date collected, and limitation. Separate "verified" from "assumption" in every table.
