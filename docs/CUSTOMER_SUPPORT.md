# Customer support

How the team answers people. Support staff use the admin portal with the `support` role: account metadata,
plans and feedback only. They can never see answers, journals, goals, reports or coaching conversations, and
must not ask people to send them.

## Channels

| Channel | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Help page (`/help`) | Self-service answers; keep it in step with this document |
| Feedback form (`/feedback`) | Problems and ideas; triaged daily during the beta (docs/BETA_PROGRAM.md) |
| Support email (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL`) | Account, payment and privacy requests; reply within 2 working days |

## Verifying who is writing

Only act on an account for requests from its registered email address. Never change an email address, grant
access or share account details because someone asks by another route. If in doubt, ask the person to sign in
and send feedback with contact allowed.

## Common requests

| Request | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Can't sign in | Point to "Forgot password". If the email never arrives, check the email provider's logs for bounces |
| Confirmation email missing | Ask them to check spam and request a new link from the sign-in page |
| Delete my account | They can do it from the account page. If they can't sign in, they reset their password first. Staff have no delete tool, by design; for an urgent case, disable the account from the admin portal and help them through the reset |
| Send me my data | They can download it from the account page; otherwise verify identity and ask counsel about the deadline (one month under GDPR) |
| Cancel or refund | Web subscriptions: the account page opens the Stripe portal. App purchases: Apple and Google handle refunds. Goodwill: grant complimentary Premium from the admin portal with a reason |
| Result seems wrong | Explain how scores work (help page and `/big-five`); never change a result. If scoring might really be wrong, treat it as a SEV2 defect |
| Asking for diagnosis or therapy | Explain the app isn't clinical; suggest a qualified professional; share crisis resources if there is any risk |
| Crisis or self-harm | Reply with the crisis resources from the help page straight away, and tell the incident lead |
| Employer wants staff results | Refuse: results belong to the person and are never for hiring or performance decisions. Organisations only see group statistics from members who opt in |
| Legal or regulator request | Pass to the owner and counsel; don't reply yourself |

## Reply style

Plain, warm and short. Never quote someone's private content back to them, never guess about their
personality, and never promise outcomes from using the app.

## Records

Keep support emails only as long as needed to resolve the request and no longer than feedback (a year). Don't
copy them into other tools.
