## Candidate differentiation opportunities (hypotheses to validate)

1. Public, versioned scoring method with audit hashes on every result.
2. Big Five foundation with plain-language explanation, rather than fixed types.
3. Archetypes presented as blends with the traits behind them shown.
4. Quality-of-response notes (rushed or uniform answers) shown to the user.
5. Coaching grounded only in the user's own results, with sources labelled.
6. Clear "not for hiring" stance as a trust signal.
7. Device-only anonymous mode before any signup.
8. Resume on any device after signup, with autosave that survives poor networks.
9. Retake comparison focused on change over time, not a new label.
10. Couples/teams sharing with explicit two-way consent and revocation.
11. No compatibility percentages; discussion prompts instead.
12. Growth programs tied to the specific trait the user wants to work on.
13. Habit and journal features that stay private from admins and employers.
14. Accessible by default (WCAG 2.2 AA tested in CI).
15. Fair pricing without dark patterns or artificial urgency.
16. Data export in open formats.
17. Amharic and other under-served languages, with reviewed translations.
18. Coach-facing tools for professionals using voluntary client sharing.
19. Enterprise insights that are aggregate-only by design.
20. Offline-tolerant mobile assessment.
21. Evidence page that states plainly what is and isn't validated.

Each item needs evidence from R1–R7 in MARKET_RESEARCH.md before it shapes the roadmap.
