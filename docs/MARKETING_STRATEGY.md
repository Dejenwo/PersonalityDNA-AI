# Marketing strategy

**Status: draft hypotheses; no spend authorized.**

- Positioning: "The personality test that shows its work, then helps you grow." Transparency and practical growth over labels.
- Organic: free assessment landing pages, educational articles on the Big Five (with citations), user-initiated share cards, referral links (both sides benefit, no spam).
- Retention: weekly reflection prompts the user opts into, growth programs, retake comparisons.
- Channels to test: SEO, short-form video explaining traits, partnerships with career coaches.
- Measurement: privacy-respecting analytics (aggregate, consented), funnel from landing → start → finish → account → subscription.
- Rules: no manipulative engagement, no sharing of private results without explicit action, no claims of scientific validation.
